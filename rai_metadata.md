# RAI Metadata Draft

## dataLimitations
This public-safe release does not include the full underlying market data or processed benchmark tensors, so it supports artifact audit and table/figure reproduction rather than full raw-data reconstruction. The benchmark itself is limited to a fixed 90-day crypto microstructure setting and should not be generalized beyond that scope without additional evidence.

## dataBiases
The released evidence inherits the benchmark's domain focus on BTC/ETH and Binance/OKX over one fixed period. It may reflect market-structure biases specific to those assets, venues, and dates.

## personalSensitiveInformation
No personal data is intentionally included. The release consists of benchmark metadata, normalized result evidence, scripts, and paper artifacts.

## dataUseCases
Supported uses: benchmark inspection, paper-audit reproduction, claim-to-evidence review, and method-comparison auditing within the published claim boundary. Unsupported uses: raw market-data redistribution and claims of deployable live trading performance from the released evidence alone.

## dataSocialImpact
Positive impact includes more reproducible evaluation for sequential decision systems. Potential negative impact includes over-interpreting normalized benchmark evidence as actionable trading advice.

## hasSyntheticData
False

## wasDerivedFrom
Derived from licensed third-party market data not redistributed in this release.

## wasGeneratedBy
Generated by PEG-Bench packaging, evaluation, and artifact-reproduction scripts over licensed source data.
