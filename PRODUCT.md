# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, reviewers, and practitioners evaluating sequential decision systems. They arrive from a paper citation or GitHub link and need to understand the benchmark thesis quickly, inspect the evidence, download the paper, and find the public-safe artifact boundary.

## Product Purpose

The PEG-Bench research site is the public front door for the Prediction--Execution Gap Benchmark. It explains why an offline prediction leaderboard is not necessarily a deployment leaderboard, makes the six-panel evidence legible, and routes visitors to the paper, evaluator code, normalized evidence, and reproduction scripts.

## Brand Personality

Precise, adversarial, accountable. The site should feel like a research instrument: confident about what is measured, explicit about what is not claimed, and generous with evidence.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative AI gradients, oversized marketing claims, fake metric cards, and claims of tradable alpha or production readiness. Do not imply that the public package reconstructs licensed raw market data.

## Design Principles

1. Show the evaluation ladder before explaining the method.
2. Put the prediction-to-execution contradiction in the first viewport.
3. Use the actual paper evidence as the visual language.
4. Separate completed evidence, diagnostics, and release boundaries.
5. Keep every path to the paper, artifact, and source code one click away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for the static site. Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, descriptive image alt text, reduced-motion defaults, responsive tables, and text equivalents for every chart claim. The site must remain usable without JavaScript.
